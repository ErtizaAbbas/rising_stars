Therapies for every child

Each child gets a plan built around their own needs, delivered by our team of therapists.

ABA Therapy

Applied Behaviour Analysis to build communication, learning and everyday skills step by step.

Behaviour Therapy

Positive strategies that help children manage emotions and behaviour at school and at home.

Speech Therapy

Support with speech, language and communication so every child can be understood.

Occupational Therapy

Fine and gross motor skills, sensory processing and self-care for daily independence.

Remedial Therapy

Focused help with reading, writing and maths for children with learning difficulties.

Art Therapy

Creative expression through art to build confidence, focus and emotional wellbeing.

Our founder

Mrs. Sarwat Zehra — a champion for inclusive education

A remedial therapist, teacher trainer, behaviour management specialist and parental counsellor with 24 years in special needs, Mrs. Sarwat holds a degree in Special Education from the University of Karachi. She founded Rising Stars in 2004 and has since trained hundreds of educators and guided countless families.

“Every child deserves a chance to shine. Together, we can create a more inclusive society where every child can reach their full potential.”— Sarwat Zehra
Pakistan Council for Social Welfare Award, 2010 Karachi Rotary Club Humanitarian Award, 2015

Our vision

We believe, practise and inculcate the mantra “Everyone is an achiever”. Every individual, however differently abled, is blessed with the means to make their life better through will and hard work.

Our mission

To facilitate and conduct vocational training that empowers individuals with the skills to run the daily affairs of their lives with confidence and independence.
